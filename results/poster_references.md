## The list, with the two additions

Numbered so the new entries follow the three already on the poster, which
keeps the existing superscripts valid. If the list is numbered by order of
first citation instead, Nakashima 2013 precedes Ueda 2026 and everything
after it shifts by one.

1. Li, X. Z., Plesiat, P. & Nikaido, H. The challenge of efflux-mediated
   antibiotic resistance in Gram-negative bacteria. Clin Microbiol Rev 28,
   337-418, (2015).
2. Sennhauser G, Bukowska MA, Briand C, Grutter MG (2009) Crystal structure of
   the multidrug exporter MexB from Pseudomonas aeruginosa. J Mol Biol
   389:134-145.
3. Ueda Y, Yonehara R, Ishizaka-Ikeda E, Nakagawa A, Yamashita E (2026)
   Structure of chloramphenicol-bound MexB reveals residues in the distal
   binding pocket that are critical for substrate recognition. J Biochem
   179:403-411.
4. Nakashima R, Sakurai K, Yamasaki S, Hayashi K, Nagata C, Hoshino K,
   Onodera Y, Nishino K, Yamaguchi A (2013) Structural basis for the
   inhibition of bacterial multidrug exporters. Nature 500:102-106.
5. Sakurai K, Yamasaki S, Nakao K, Nishino K, Yamaguchi A, Nakashima R (2019)
   Crystal structures of multidrug efflux pump MexB bound with
   high-molecular-mass compounds. Sci Rep 9:4359.

## Note on 3W9I against 3W9J

Both come from Nakashima 2013 and both carry DDM, and their PDB titles are
identical, so the title cannot tell them apart. Only 3W9J carries the
inhibitor P9D. Worth confirming which of the two the second DDM column was
rendered from.
